🚧 Project Specification: The DOT Insider (MVP)
1. Project Overview

The DOT Insider is a professional web app that delivers regulatory updates, educational resources, and verified reviews for DOT-mandated substance abuse education providers.
The goal is to create a trusted, centralized hub for SAPs, EAPs, and compliance professionals.

Core Objectives:

Provide up-to-date regulatory and clearinghouse news.

Offer educational resources related to SAP roles and return-to-duty (RTD) processes.

Host independent reviews and rankings of education providers.

Connect SAPs to reputable, DOT-relevant organizations (e.g., ASAP, The SAP List, ReCourse.life).

Position The DOT Insider as a neutral, authoritative publication — not a vendor site.

2. Target Audience

Primary: Substance Abuse Professionals (SAPs)

Secondary: EAPs, DERs, and compliance officers at safety-sensitive employers

Tertiary: Education providers and industry associations

3. Core Features (MVP)
