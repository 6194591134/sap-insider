---
name: Regulatory news accuracy
description: Editorial safeguards when refreshing regulatory news.
---

Verify regulatory news against official primary sources, preserve real publication dates, and distinguish petitions, proposals, guidance, and final rules. Clearly mark elapsed comment deadlines.

**Why:** Earlier generated articles asserted unsupported effective rules, testing availability, and rate changes. These claims could mislead readers making compliance decisions.

**How to apply:** Before publishing or replacing regulatory stories, read the underlying agency notice and attach official sources. Do not invent newer dates or describe a proposal as effective law just to make the feed look current.
